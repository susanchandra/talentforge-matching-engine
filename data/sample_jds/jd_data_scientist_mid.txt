Mid-Level Data Scientist
Company: DataDriven Analytics
Location: Mumbai / Hybrid
Experience: 3+ years

About the Role:
We are seeking a skilled Data Scientist to join our Analytics & Insights team.
You will work across multiple business units to extract insights from complex
datasets, build predictive models, and communicate findings to stakeholders.
This role sits at the intersection of analytics, ML, and business strategy.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design and implement statistical models and machine learning solutions
  to solve business problems across marketing, operations, and product
- Conduct exploratory data analysis and hypothesis testing on large datasets
- Build dashboards and automated reports for business stakeholders
- Work with data engineers to define data pipelines and feature requirements
- Present findings and model results to non-technical audiences
- Continuously monitor model performance in production

Required Skills:
- Python (scikit-learn, pandas, numpy, matplotlib)
- Statistical modeling: regression, classification, clustering
- SQL (complex queries, window functions, CTEs)
- Experiment design and A/B testing
- Data visualization (Tableau, Power BI, or Plotly)
- Basic understanding of machine learning pipelines
- Experience with at least one cloud platform (AWS, GCP, or Azure)

Preferred Skills:
- Experience with NLP or time-series forecasting
- Apache Spark for big data processing
- MLflow for experiment tracking
- Bayesian methods or causal inference
- Knowledge of marketing analytics or customer segmentation

Seniority Level: Mid (3-5 years)
Role Type: Data Scientist
Domain: Data Science / Analytics
Team Size: 8-person analytics team

Key Expectations:
- Deliver actionable insights from data with clear business impact
- Build and deploy predictive models with proper documentation
- Communicate complex findings clearly to non-technical stakeholders
- Collaborate across functions to define success metrics
- Maintain data quality standards and reproducible analysis workflows
